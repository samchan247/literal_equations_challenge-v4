Linear vs Literal Equations: Step-by-Step Parallel Presentation

📊 Slide 1: One-Step Equations
Level 1
🎯 Objective: Master the "Inverse Operation" to isolate w.

🟢 Numerical Linear Equation

Solve for x:

x + 5 = 12
  • To get x by itself, eliminate the +5.
  • Subtract 5 from both sides of the equation: x = 12 - 5
Final Answer: x = 7

🔵 Literal Equation

Make w as the subject of the formula:

w + a = b
  • To get w by itself, eliminate the +a.
  • Subtract 'a' from both sides of the equation: w = b - a
Final Answer: w = b - a
💡 Golden Rule: Moving a term across the equal sign changes its sign (positive becomes negative, and vice versa).
📊 Slide 2: Two-Step Equations
Level 2
🎯 Objective: Clear the constant term first (addition/subtraction), then eliminate the coefficient (multiplication/division).

🟢 Numerical Linear Equation

Solve for x:

3x - 4 = 11
  • Move the constant: Add 4 to both sides: 3x = 11 + 4 → 3x = 15
  • Isolate x: Divide both sides by 3: x = 15 / 3
Final Answer: x = 5

🔵 Literal Equation

Make y as the subject of the formula:

ay - b = c
  • Move the constant: Add 'b' to both sides: ay = c + b
  • Isolate y: Divide both sides by 'a': y = (c + b) / a
Final Answer: y = (c + b) / a (where a ≠ 0)
💡 Golden Rule: Transforming literal equations is like peeling an onion. Work from the outside in—move the loose numbers first, then divide by the coefficient attached to the target variable.
📊 Slide 3: Equations with Parentheses
Level 3
🎯 Objective: Apply the Distributive Property correctly and keep track of your variables.

🟢 Numerical Linear Equation

Solve for x:

2(x - 3) = 8
  • Expand: Distribute 2 into the parentheses: 2x - 6 = 8
  • Move constant: Add 6 to both sides: 2x = 8 + 6 → 2x = 14
  • Isolate x: Divide both sides by 2
Final Answer: x = 7

🔵 Literal Equation

Make z as the subject of the formula:

a(z - b) = c
  • Expand: Distribute 'a' into the parentheses: az - ab = c
  • Move constant: Add 'ab' to both sides: az = c + ab
  • Isolate z: Divide both sides by 'a': z = (c + ab) / a
Final Answer: z = (c + ab) / a (where a ≠ 0)
💡 Golden Rule: When distributing, the multiplier outside acts like rain—everything inside the parentheses must get hit by it!
📊 Slide 4: Variables on Both Sides
Level 4
🎯 Objective: Gather all terms with the target variable on the left side and all other terms on the right side.

🟢 Numerical Linear Equation

Solve for x:

5x - 2 = 2x + 7
  • Group terms: Move 2x left, move -2 right: 5x - 2x = 7 + 2
  • Combine like terms: Simply subtract numbers: 3x = 9
  • Isolate x: Divide both sides by 3
Final Answer: x = 3

🔵 Literal Equation

Make m as the subject of the formula:

am - c = bm + d
  • Group terms: Move bm left, move -c right: am - bm = d + c
  • KEY STEP: Factor out 'm' using parentheses: m(a - b) = d + c
  • Isolate m: Divide by the group (a - b)
Final Answer: m = (d + c) / (a - b) (where a ≠ b)
💡 Golden Rule: Factoring out m to get m(a - b) in a literal equation is conceptually identical to combining 5x - 2x to get 3x in a numerical one!
📊 Slide 5: Equations with Fractions
Level 5
🎯 Objective: Use the Least Common Multiple (LCM) to eliminate denominators and convert fractions into integers.

🟢 Numerical Linear Equation

Solve for x:

x/2 + x/3 = 5
  • Clear fractions: The LCM of 2 and 3 is 6. Multiply every term by 6: 3x + 2x = 30
  • Combine like terms: 5x = 30
  • Isolate x: Divide by 5
Final Answer: x = 6

🔵 Literal Equation

Make n as the subject of the formula:

n/a + n/b = c
  • Clear fractions: The common multiple is ab. Multiply every term by ab: bn + an = abc
  • KEY STEP: Factor out 'n': n(b + a) = abc
  • Isolate n: Divide by the group (b + a)
Final Answer: n = abc / (a + b) (where a,b ≠ 0, a ≠ -b)
💡 Golden Rule: If fractions look intimidating, destroy them immediately by multiplying the entire equation by the common denominator.
📊 Slide 6: The Ultimate Challenge
Level 6
🎯 Objective: Synthesize cross-multiplication, expansion, grouping, and factoring into one master sequence.

🟢 Numerical Linear Equation

Solve for x:

(2x + 1)/3 = (x - 2)/2
  • Cross-Multiply: Multiply all by 6: 2(2x + 1) = 3(x - 2)
  • Expand parentheses: 4x + 2 = 3x - 6
  • Group terms: x on left, constants on right: 4x - 3x = -6 - 2
Final Answer: x = -8

🔵 Literal Equation

Make p as the subject of the formula:

(p + a)/b = (p - b)/a
  • Cross-Multiply: Multiply all by ab: a(p + a) = b(p - b)
  • Expand parentheses: ap + a² = bp - b²
  • Group terms: p on left, variables on right: ap - bp = -b² - a²
  • Factor out p and isolate: p(a - b) = -(a² + b²)
Final Answer: p = (a² + b²) / (b - a) (where a ≠ b)
💡 Golden Rule: No matter how complicated a literal equation looks, it follows the exact same 4-step workflow: Clear Denominators → Clear Parentheses → Group Like Terms → Factor & Divide.
📊 Slide 7: Unknown in the Denominator (One Side)
Level 7
🎯 Objective: Rescue the target variable from the denominator when it appears on only one side of the equation.

🟢 Numerical Linear Equation

Solve for x:

6/x + 2 = 5
  • Isolate the fraction: Subtract 2 from both sides: 6/x = 5 - 2 → 6/x = 3
  • Clear the denominator: Multiply both sides by x: 6 = 3x
  • Isolate x: Divide both sides by 3: x = 6 / 3
Final Answer: x = 2

🔵 Literal Equation

Make q as the subject of the formula:

a/q + b = c
  • Isolate the fraction: Subtract b from both sides: a/q = c - b
  • Clear the denominator: Multiply both sides by q: a = q(c - b)
  • Isolate q: Divide both sides by the group (c - b): q = a / (c - b)
Final Answer: q = a / (c - b) (where q ≠ 0, c ≠ b)
💡 Golden Rule: Never try to isolate the variable while it is trapped downstairs! Multiply to bring it upstairs first.
📊 Slide 8: Unknown in the Denominator (Both Sides)
Level 8
🎯 Objective: Use cross-multiplication or common denominators to gather and factor out the variable when it appears in multiple denominators.

🟢 Numerical Linear Equation

Solve for x:

3/x = 5/(x + 2)
  • Cross-Multiply: Multiply across the equal sign: 3(x + 2) = 5x
  • Expand: Distribute the 3: 3x + 6 = 5x
  • Group terms: Move 3x to the right side to keep x positive: 6 = 5x - 3x → 6 = 2x
  • Isolate x: Divide by 2: x = 6 / 2
Final Answer: x = 3

🔵 Literal Equation

Make r as the subject of the formula:

a/r = b/(r + c)
  • Cross-Multiply: Multiply across the equal sign: a(r + c) = br
  • Expand: Distribute the 'a': ar + ac = br
  • Group terms: Move all r terms to one side: ac = br - ar
  • KEY STEP: Factor out 'r': ac = r(b - a)
  • Isolate r: Divide by the group (b - a): r = ac / (b - a)
Final Answer: r = ac / (b - a) (where r ≠ 0, r ≠ -c, a ≠ b)
💡 Golden Rule: Cross-multiplication is your best weapon against fractions. It instantly transforms a scary rational equation into a familiar linear layout!
